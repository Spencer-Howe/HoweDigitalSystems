01 · Flagship

The Howe Ranch booking platform

The registration and ticketing system for a Kalispell agritourism ranch. I've been building it since 2024 and it runs the business: guests pick an experience, choose a date and time, pay online, sign their waiver, and get their confirmation and reminders without anyone picking up the phone.

  • Date and time slots with capacity
  • PayPal payments confirmed by webhook
  • Digital waivers and signed agreement PDFs
  • Private events: request, approve, pay
  • Gift cards
  • Confirmations, reminders and follow-ups
  • QR passes, scanned by a staff Android app
  • Admin dashboard for the whole operation

02 · For the valley

406 KAPLOW!

Snowed in? Not anymore, sucker.

A free board I built for the Flathead that connects people who are snowed in with people who plow and shovel. Plows show up on a 3D map made from real elevation and road data, drivers design their own badge and sign in with an emailed link, and approved drivers get an email when a driveway is posted in their town. The mascot is Gary. Gary is a snow berm.

03 · Try it yourself

Trip Scheduler for traveling hoof care

Replaces phone contacts, notes and one-by-one texts. Customers sign up once and get grouped by area, the owner plans a trip and sends dates in one click, customers tap to confirm, and the route sheet builds itself.

The demo runs on fake Montana customers, catches every email, and resets every hour. Plan a trip, hit send, break whatever you like.
