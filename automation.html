Case Study · Field Service Scheduling

Trip Scheduler for a traveling hoof trimmer

A shearer and hoof trimmer covering hundreds of miles of rural country, with customers whose alpacas, sheep, goats and pigs need to be seen a few times a year. Shearing runs March through August, trims fill in around it, and she heads to each area based on weather and demand.

Before
  • Contacts saved in her phone as “location · name · animals” so she could search by town
  • Route lists kept in iPhone Notes, the only record of where she'd been and who she saw
  • Texting dates to customers one by one, then tracking who answered in her head
  • No real plan, just fitting trims before and after shearing season
After
  • Customers sign up once with their zip and get grouped by area automatically
  • Plan a run by picking an area and dates, then send everyone there a heads-up in one click
  • Closer to the date, send firm dates. Customers tap a link to confirm and add notes
  • A ready-made route sheet of who's in, with animals, gate codes and notes. People who text get one tap: Confirm
1

Customers sign up once

A simple mobile page she can share anywhere: a text, social media, a business card. Customers enter their zip and animals, and the app files them into the right area using zip-prefix matching. “599” covers the whole Flathead, and a full zip can override it.

2

Plan a trip and send a heads-up

Pick an area and type the dates. Everyone signed up there gets a personal “we're planning to be in your area” email. Press send again later and it only reaches new sign-ups, so nobody gets emailed twice.

3

Firm dates and one-tap confirm

Each customer gets their own private link, already filled in with their details. They tap Yes, count me in, update their animals, and leave notes for this visit, like how many head or a gate code.

4

The route sheet builds itself

Confirmed customers land on the trip page with phone, zip, animals and visit notes, which is her route for the day. Anyone still waiting is listed underneath, and if they reply by text she taps Confirm. Every trip is saved, so “where did I go last fall?” is one click away.

Searchable customer list. Her list is hers: Export → CSV any time.

She draws her own areas by zip. Change one and every customer is regrouped instantly.

Under the Hood

Small app, built properly.

PythonFlaskSQLAlchemy Flask-AdminFlask-LoginSMTP / Gmail Docker ComposeGunicornCloudflare Tunnel pytest
  • Clean data model. Customers, Areas, Trips and Stops. A stop tracks each customer through projected → firm → confirmed or declined.
  • Safe to press twice. Sends are idempotent: re-sending only emails people who haven't had that step, never someone who already answered.
  • Private confirm links. Each stop gets a random URL-safe token, so customers confirm without accounts or passwords.
  • Real email, no extra service. Batched through her own Gmail with one SMTP connection per send, so failures are reported and can be retried.
  • Deployed with no open ports. Dockerized behind a Cloudflare Tunnel and HTTPS, with an automated test suite covering the full trip flow.
Case Study · Booking & Payments

Howe Ranch booking platform

The full registration and ticketing system for a Kalispell agritourism ranch, live and taking real bookings. Guests pick an experience, choose a date and time, pay online and get their confirmation and waiver automatically. Staff run it all from an admin dashboard.

  • Events & ticketing. Admin sets up events and ticket pricing, and manages every booking.
  • PayPal API payments, with webhooks confirming each booking.
  • Automated emails for confirmations and waiver requests via Flask-Mail.
  • Private events hidden from the public site and shared by exclusive link.
  • Android check-in app (Kotlin) scans guests' QR codes at the gate through a REST API.
