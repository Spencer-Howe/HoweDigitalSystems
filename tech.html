What I Can Help With

From the dish on the roof to the laptop on the desk.

Starlink installs

Mounting the dish, routing the cable cleanly, putting the router where it does the most good, and getting Wi-Fi out to the house, shop or barn.

Routers & Wi-Fi

Router setup or replacement, access points for dead spots, guest Wi-Fi, VLANs to keep cameras and smart devices on their own network, and VPNs so you can reach home from anywhere.

Network cabling

Running Ethernet to the office, shop, outbuildings or camera spots, so the things that matter are wired instead of fighting for Wi-Fi.

Security cameras

IP cameras with local recording and AI person and vehicle detection, alerts to your phone and a simple dashboard. No monthly cloud subscription. I run this setup at my own property.

Home labs & media servers

Home servers, Jellyfin media servers and self-hosted apps in Docker, set up on hardware you own and explained so you're not stuck with a black box.

Computer builds & buying help

Custom PC builds, and help picking a laptop or desktop that fits what you actually do, so you don't pay for more than you need.

Phone & computer help

New device setup, moving your stuff over, and troubleshooting. On Android I can go deeper over ADB when the normal settings won't do it.

Basic data recovery

Getting photos and files off a failing drive or phone when it can be done. If it needs a clean-room lab, I'll tell you straight and point you to one.

Setting up a whole property

New place or rural property? I'll plan it start to finish: internet in, Wi-Fi where you need it, cameras on the gate and shop, and everything labeled so you know how it works.
