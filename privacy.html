Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Howe Digital Systems ("we," "us") collects and uses information when you visit howedigitalsystems.com, contact us, or receive text messages from us.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, business name and project details when you fill out our contact form, call, text or email us.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit the site, such as browser type and pages viewed.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, deliver and support the services you request, send invoices and reminders, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you text our business number or agree to receive texts from us, we use your mobile number to communicate about your inquiry, appointments, project updates and invoices. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our phone provider), and only for that purpose.

Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to run our business, such as website hosting, form handling, email, phone/text and payment processing. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their service.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us below. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
